# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want Nexus and its Winter of Code community to be welcoming and respectful for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We are committed to providing a friendly, safe, and inclusive environment for all participants.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, patient, and respectful.
- Welcome newcomers and help them participate.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its community.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, and make a genuine effort to learn from them.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or abusive conduct; sexualized language or unwelcome advances; personal attacks, insults, or deliberate disruption; publishing another person's private information without permission; and any other conduct that would reasonably make someone feel unsafe or unwelcome.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and other community channels, as well as when someone is representing the project or its community in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the project maintainers through the contact method listed in the repository profile. Please include what happened, when and where it happened, and any relevant links or context. Share only information you are comfortable providing.

Maintainers will review reports fairly and confidentially, to the extent practical, and will take action appropriate to the circumstances. They may remove or edit contributions, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or exclude someone from the project or community. Maintainers will communicate the outcome to the reporter when possible.

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct. They are expected to apply it fairly and consistently.
